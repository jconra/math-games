# The cabin's furniture: credits

Stand-ins until there are made-to-measure models: each file fills one slot in src/cabin/slots.js, which fits it to its
spot whatever its size, so a new model replaces one by taking its file name. All from Poly Haven, whose assets are all
CC0 (public domain, no attribution required): <https://polyhaven.com/license>. Downloaded 2026-10-12 at 1k, textures
re-encoded as WebP (colour 1024 or 512, the rest 512) by ~/pw/bandit-math/rigs/cabin_furniture.mjs. 1.3 MB in all.

| File | Model | Page | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| table.glb | Wooden Table 02 | <https://polyhaven.com/a/wooden_table_02> | CC0 |
| chair.glb | Painted Wooden Chair 01 | <https://polyhaven.com/a/painted_wooden_chair_01> | CC0 |
| picture.glb | Fancy Picture Frame 01 | <https://polyhaven.com/a/fancy_picture_frame_01> | CC0 |
| porch_chair.glb | Rocking Chair 01 | <https://polyhaven.com/a/Rockingchair_01> | CC0 |

bed.glb (the quilted log bed) and stove.glb (the iron pot-belly stove) are made for this game in Tripo by its author, and
share this repository's licence; ~/pw/bandit-math/rigs/furniture_tripo.mjs re-encodes their textures as WebP and narrows
the bed to a single. lantern.glb is the oil lantern hanging on the tent of camp 3 (also the author's, made in Tripo),
cut out with its own small picture by ~/pw/bandit-math/rigs/lantern_from_camp3.mjs.

The rug, the lanterns' light and cords, the dog beds, the rifle rack (with the game's own rifle and shotgun on it), the stove's fire and pipe, and the belt-buckle keepsakes are made in code
(src/cabin/pieces.js).
