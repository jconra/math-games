W A S D fly the view forward, left, back and right, along the ground the way it faces; Space up, C down; hold Shift to go faster. Drag the ground to turn the view, right-drag (or two fingers) to slide it, wheel or pinch to zoom.

The bar along the bottom has everything: Select (then Move or Turn, Snap to half metres and 15°), Add, Clear, every building, prop, fence, gate, path, tree and flower, Undo and Redo. Picking a fence or a flower shows its choices just over the bar (a fence's style and height, Gate, Take down; Paint or Rub out).

Ctrl+S saves, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo, Delete takes the chosen thing away.
