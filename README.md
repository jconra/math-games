This is a project for random math games that can be played in the browser. The goal is to help people who want to practice some math and have fun.

## Credits

### Bandit Math

- Farm animals (the cow, horse, pig, sheep and the pug you can buy as a dog): [LowPoly Animated Farm Animal Pack](https://opengameart.org/node/85047) by Quaternius, CC0. Exported from the pack's .blend files; the pig, sheep and pug walks were refitted to their shorter legs, and the pug's sit, lie and trot were made for the game.
- The cabin's furniture stand-ins (models/furniture/, see its CREDITS.md): the bed ([Vintage Day Bed](https://polyhaven.com/a/vintage_day_bed)), the table ([Wooden Table 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/wooden_table_02)), the chairs ([Painted Wooden Chair 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/painted_wooden_chair_01)), the painting ([Fancy Picture Frame 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/fancy_picture_frame_01)) and the porch rockers ([Rocking Chair 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/Rockingchair_01)), all from Poly Haven, CC0. The rug, lanterns, dog beds, rifle rack, stove and the keepsakes are made in code.
- The stage music (harmonica and piano) is made in the browser as it plays: no recordings. The two instruments are patches for the [Sound Lab](https://github.com/jconra/sound-lab) engine, and the tune is made up as it goes.
- Cowboy voice clips (the player's "Hyah!", "Whoa", "Yeehaw!", "Howdy" and the rest, and the townsmen's and bandits' "Much obliged", "You got me" and so on, bandit-math/sounds/voice/): recorded by the game's maker.
- The town's animal and bird sounds (hens, a rooster, cows, sheep, horses, the dogs, a meadowlark, crows, ravens, a hawk) are short recordings, every one CC0 or public domain, from [BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com) (Joseph SARDIN and contributors), the US National Park Service's Yellowstone sound library, Wikimedia Commons and OpenGameArt.org: each clip with its source page, author and licence in bandit-math/sounds/CREDITS.md. The hammering and sawing out back, the hooves, the creek and the pigs' grunts are made in the browser, no recordings.
- The action music for "Protect the wagon" (the galloping drums, plucked bass, twangy guitar, whistled tune, whip cracks and the big drum on a catch) is made in the browser as it plays too: seven Sound Lab patches, a tune made up as it goes, no recordings.
- The hats at the general store: the cowboy and white hats are the farmer's hat from Quaternius' characters (CC0); the straw hat, bowler, ranger hat, cattleman and sombrero are shaped in code. The horses at the stable are the Quaternius horses, donkey and farm-pack horse already in the game, some with their coats tinted.
- The camps out in the valley (models/camps/, see its CREDITS.md): the cow camp, the tipi camp and the surveyors' wall tent are the game maker's own models, made with Tripo; each was joined into one shape, sized to life and its picture shrunk for the game.
- The wagons on the road (models/wagons/, see its CREDITS.md): the covered wagon, the chuck wagon, the farm wagon and the freight wagon are the game maker's own models, made with Tripo; each was joined into one shape, given one shared wheel (rebuilt from the farm wagon's back wheel) that turns, sized to life and its picture shrunk for the game. The teams are the farm-pack horse (above); the harness is made in code. The rumble, creaks and hooves are made in the browser (three Sound Lab patches), no recordings.
- The wild deer and the stag: the deer and stag from the [Ultimate Animated Animal Pack](https://poly.pizza/bundle/Animated-Animal-Pack-ILAPXeUYiS) by Quaternius, CC0. Old Hob's box camera on its tripod (with its flash and puff of smoke) is made in code; the shutter's click and the flash powder's whoomp are made in the browser, no recordings.
- The campfires' flames: [VolumetricFire](https://github.com/yomotsu/VolumetricFire) by Akihiro Oyamada (yomotsu), MIT licence, ported from the [WebGL fire demo](http://webgl-fire.appspot.com/html/fire.html); src/fire/VolumetricFire.js with its two pictures (firetex.png, nzw.png), by way of the Shipwrecked game's campfire and the jconra.com fire lab. The crackle is made in the browser (two Sound Lab patches), no recordings.
