# The camps: credits

- **camp1.glb** (the cow camp: an A-frame tent, a ring of stones with a pot on a tripod, stumps, a barrel, a saddle on a
  rail, a coil of rope), **camp2.glb** (the tipi camp: a painted tipi, an awning with pots, a ring of stones, a cart
  wheel, logs) and **camp3.glb** (a big wall tent): the game maker's own models, made with Tripo.
- Built for the game by `~/pw/bandit-math/rigs/camps/build_camps.mjs` (not in this repo): every part joined into one
  shape (one draw a camp), sized to life (camp1 5.8 m deep, camp2 7 m across, camp3 5.4 m square), the middle over the
  origin and the lowest point on the ground, the 4096 picture shrunk to 2048 (here) and 1024 (`lo/`, for the normal and
  potato tiers), meshopt-compressed. `camps.json` lists each camp's size and its fire ring's middle, in metres.
