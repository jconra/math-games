# Cabin with an inside: credits

## The model

- **Log cabin** (`models/town/cabin_inside.glb`, from `cabinFixed.glb`): made with Tripo and fixed by hand in Blender (the
  window openings cut out, the door cut out of the wall, moved into the doorway, fitted and named "Door"). Its outside,
  and Tripo's picture for it, are kept as they were, except the door, which has the plank door picture on both faces, and
  the porch (its floor, the front wall's outer face under the porch roof, and the porch roof's underside and joists), which
  uses the same CC0 pictures as the inside, tinted to Tripo's colour there.

# The mill on the mill hill

- **The mill** (`models/town/mill.glb`, and `models/town/lo/mill.glb` with a 1024 picture for the normal and potato
  tiers): made for this game with Meshy (image to 3D from a concept picture made for the game, remeshed to 13.7k
  triangles, the loose pieces tidied in Blender). Real size: 16 by 12 feet, 9.4 feet to the ridge; its porch runs along
  one long side (+Z), its door is in the gable end at -X.
- The flour sacks stacked on its porch are made in code (src/windmilljob.js), from the same shape as the loot's sacks.
